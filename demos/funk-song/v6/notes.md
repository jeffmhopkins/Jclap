Rendered from main at 36aac23 (JDrummer 0.2.0 with the funk fixes, JKit 0.8.0). The drums come out
note for note the same as v5's build from the PR.

## Lead changes from v5
- **Comes in at bar 9 (0:19),** after the e-piano has had four bars of verse 1 alone.
- **One melody per section,** repeated so it sticks: a two-bar verse phrase (B A G A, B G E), lifting
  over G9 and A9 into the chorus; a four-bar chorus hook (E C# A, G A C# E, D B G A, B), played
  again and turned on B7#9; the break chants E D B twice with a pickup; the outro is the verse
  phrase and a fall to E.
- **Longer between words:** fewer, longer notes, and Alien Talk down from 0.6 to 0.1, so a held
  note keeps its syllable instead of chattering on to the next.
- **Unison and reverb:** Unison 2 (detune 10 cents), Room 0.45 (size 0.7, decay 2.6 s), and a light
  delay (mix 0.14).
