# Funk song v4

v3c plus an electric piano: JArp's comping presets into JSynth's *Tine Piano*.

| Section | JArp preset | Voicings held (rootless 9ths over the root) |
|---|---|---|
| Intro | (out) | |
| Verse | Neo Soul Comp (laid-back pushes) | E9, G9, A9 |
| Chorus | Gospel Comp (swung sixteenth stabs) | A9 A9 E9 E9 A9 A9 B7#9 B7#9 |
| Break, outro | Neo Soul Comp | E9 |
| End | E9 left to ring | |

The comping presets also play the root low; that's the bass's job, so notes under C3 are dropped.
In the mix the e-piano sits under the bass, a little right of centre, with a short reverb tail
(1.4 s of decaying noise, in the mix script, since JSynth has no reverb of its own).
