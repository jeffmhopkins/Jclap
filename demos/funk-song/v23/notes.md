# Funk song v23

- E-piano, verse 1 and outro, articulation: each chord gets a length. Downbeats mostly ring, sometimes with the bottom notes lifted so only the top note keeps singing. The pops are mostly short dug-in chops, sometimes half-length, rarely left to ring.
- E-piano grace notes: on about 45% of the moves to a new shape, either the G natural is hammered onto the G# (minor third to major), or the top note slips in from a step below, 45-70 ms ahead.
- Ending: the outro grooves for 6 bars (bars 41-46), with the outro line sung twice, the second time higher. Then there's a 2-bar tag with the whole band in stop-time: E9 hits on 1, the "a" of 1 and the "and" of 2, G9 and A9 on 3 and 4, B7#9 on the next one, then a snare-and-tom fill. It lands on a final E9 with kick and crash at bar 49 and rings out. Length 1:59.
