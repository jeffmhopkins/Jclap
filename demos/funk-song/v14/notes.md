# Funk song v14

v13 with a sparser e-piano in verse 1 and the outro: it picks out the bass's important notes and
lets the chords ring between them, two or three a bar instead of every hit.
- Every bar: the downbeat, a 9th chord held until the next stab.
- Even bars: the bass's octave pop on the "a" of 2, as the 13th.
- Odd bars: the bass's b7 on beat 3 as the IV over the root (A/E), and its pop on beat 4 as the 13th,
  leading into the next bar.
Everything else is as v13 (bass as v10/v11, drums as v9 onward).
