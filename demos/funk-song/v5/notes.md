# Funk song v5 (100 BPM, E, 1:49)

v4's drums (JDrummer PR 285 build, JKit funk kit with Plate reverb and panning) and Finger Bass, plus:

## Lead: JBreath, Alien Speech
JBreath's Alien model (preset *Alien Speech*) singing its own made-up words, a syllable a note,
with its own room. Hosted as the real plugin. E dorian with a blue note.
- Intro: out. Verses: a two-bar call and answer, lifting over G9 and A9 into the chorus.
- Choruses: the hook, up to E5, turning on B7#9. Break: a chanted figure and a pickup.
- Outro: a falling line, ending on a held E.

## E-piano: JSynth Tine Piano, scored comp
Written out rather than from JArp's comping presets, which were too even for funk: syncopated
sixteenth stabs, ghosted chops, 9th and 13th voicings swapping, slides in from a half step below,
and a tiny roll up each chord. Each section has its own part:
- Verse 1: laid back, a stab on the "and" of one, held chords.
- Chorus 1: off-beat stabs, answered by a pushed second bar.
- Verse 2: the busy chopping comp.
- Break: two held chords with slide pickups.
- Chorus 2: up an octave, sixteenth chops, a two-bar answer.
- Outro: back to verse 1's feel, ending on E9.

`source/song.py` builds it all; `source/*.txt` are the note logs of each part.
