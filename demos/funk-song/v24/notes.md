# Funk song v24

- E-piano, verse 1 and outro: the notes of each chord start together again (within 2 ms). There's no roll and no grace notes into the chords. Each chord still moves a little off the grid as a whole, and the length changes from v23 are kept.
- Passing lines: in about half the gaps of five or more sixteenths between chords, a run of 2 to 4 single sixteenth notes walks into the next chord's top note. The runs are in E dorian, go up or down, and step in from a half step below when coming from underneath. The chord before a run is shortened so the run is heard on its own.
- The ending is the same as v23.
